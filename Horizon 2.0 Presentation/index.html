Horizon 2.0 · Design rationale

Why we chose these two, and what they will move

Critical fix · P0 · Strategic opportunity

Case lifecycle status

The status field today mashes together invite state, consent state, order state, and assessment state into one field. Nobody — including support — can interpret it reliably.

What it will move
NPS 10 today → 36+ (B2B SaaS average, 12-month target)
Status-related support unquantified → measurable reduction, baseline at launch
Status clarity ~3 ambiguous, conflated states → 9 clear, distinct lifecycle states
Confirmed as the primary source of admin confusion across every Marvin research session, including support staff. Affects every case in the product.
P0 · Strategic opportunity · #1 most requested feature¹

Invite link management

After an invite is sent, the link disappears. Email is the only channel. No way to know if it bounced. Admins manually resend 3–4 times per volunteer. Every time.

What it will move
Time to consent 5+ days (most common) → under 48 hours
Resends per applicant 3–4, manual, every case → 1 or fewer
Send channels platform email only → any channel (copy link)
Majority of orgs experience incomplete consent forms "very often." Manual follow-up is the default in every organisation.

¹ Source: PMM-045 discovery backlog (P0 · Strategic Opportunity) + Marvin survey project 53601.

Also: migrating to MB UI Library (Unity / Storybook). Current Radzen stack is WCAG non-compliant and a VPAT cannot be produced. Government-funded clients increasingly require one at renewal. MB UI Library = WCAG 2.2 AA at launch, VPAT producible, DB-to-UI security risk resolved.
