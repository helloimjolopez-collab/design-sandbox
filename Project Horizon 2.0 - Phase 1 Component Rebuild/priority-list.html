C0

Prioritisation Logic

The sequencing rationale. Each layer depends on the layer above it being stable.

OrderLayerReason
1stToken system clean-upNothing can be authoritatively tokenised until the Color migration is complete, legacy tokens removed, and light/dark mode decisions made. Building on unstable tokens creates rework that multiplies with every component added.
2ndExisting atoms with known gapsButton is already marked ❇️ and partially authored. Closing its gaps is a small effort with high payoff — every screen uses buttons, and they form the baseline confidence signal for the dev team.
3rdHigh-frequency form atomsText Input, Dropdown, Checkbox, Radio, Toggle appear on every single screen. These must be complete before any form-heavy screen (Invite, Settings) can be designed or built. They are also the prerequisite for the Wizard organism.
4thData Table organismThe dominant pattern across three primary screens. Authoring this organism is the single highest-impact deliverable — it unlocks Background Checks, Screening Flows, and Child Safety in one shot. Treat as a standalone work stream.
5thNavigation & utility atomsBadge, Tabs, Breadcrumb, Tooltip, Modal, Avatar are needed for Applicant Profile and supporting views. Lower urgency than form atoms — fewer screens depend on them.
6thRemaining organismsWizard, Settings Form Section, Screening Flow Card can only be correctly authored once their constituent atoms are stable. Sequencing after atoms avoids rework loops.
7thScreen templatesTemplates are assembled from finished organisms. Fast to produce once atoms and organisms are done — attempting them earlier would require placeholder components and create technical debt.
